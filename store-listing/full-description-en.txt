Guardian helps you understand what your apps are doing on the network—locally on your Android device, with no account required.

WHAT GUARDIAN DOES
• Monitors network connections per app using Android's VPN permission
• Records metadata only: domain names, data sizes, timestamps
• Never reads your messages, photos, or file contents
• Alerts you when activity looks unusual or suspicious
• Works fully offline—no cloud account needed

KEY FEATURES
• Home dashboard with safe / unusual / suspicious counts
• App list with risk levels and explanations
• Activity timeline and security alerts
• Block suspicious domains (DNS-based, best-effort)
• Trust apps you know are safe
• Export your data as JSON anytime
• English and Hebrew (עברית) with RTL support

PRIVACY FIRST
All processing happens on your device. Optional cloud sync is off by default. You control data retention (7–90 days). Uninstall or clear storage to delete everything.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 8+ recommended
• VPN permission required for monitoring
• Physical device (not Expo Go)

Guardian is a privacy tool, not antivirus software. It helps you make informed decisions about your apps.
